Privacy Policy

Finanțe · last updated 14 November 2026

Who we are

Finanțe is a personal, non-commercial budgeting application built and operated by Gabriel Florea, an individual in Romania. The controller for the purposes of the GDPR is Gabriel Florea, reachable at g.florea2004@gmail.com.

The application has exactly two users: the developer and his partner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Where your data is stored

All application data is stored in a single SQLite database on a server located in the developer's home in Romania. The server is reachable only over a private network (Tailscale); it is not exposed to the public internet. There is no cloud database, no hosting provider, and no analytics service.

Bank account information

Where a user chooses to connect a bank account, account and transaction data is retrieved through Enable Banking, a licensed account information service provider operating under PSD2. Enable Banking acts as our processor for this purpose.

  • Access is read-only. The application cannot move money, make payments, or change anything at the bank.
  • Access happens only after the user authenticates directly with their own bank and grants explicit consent. The application never sees or stores bank credentials.
  • Consent expires periodically under PSD2 and must be renewed by the user.
  • A user can disconnect an account at any time from the app's settings, and separately revoke consent at their bank.

From retrieved transactions the application keeps a summary — totals by category for a period. Uploaded bank statement files are read in memory and are never written to disk.

Legal basis

Processing is based on consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by each user when creating an account and, separately, when connecting a bank account. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

Who we share data with

Nobody. Data is not sold, rented, shared, or transferred to any third party. There are no advertisers, no analytics, and no tracking. The only external service involved is Enable Banking, and only for the bank connection described above.

Within the application, a strict boundary applies between the two users: shared figures are visible to both, while each person's individual spending is visible only as a total to the other, never as a list of transactions.

How long we keep it

Data is kept as long as the account exists. On request, an account and all data belonging to it are deleted from the database and from backups. Local backups are kept for a limited period and then overwritten.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and portability, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Write to g.florea2004@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian supervisory authority (ANSPDCP).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and users are told inside the application.
