Finanțe

A private, self-hosted budgeting app for two people.

Finanțe is a personal, non-commercial application. It is used by its developer and his partner to plan how they split each month's income. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not for sale.

It runs on a server in the developer's home. It is not a hosted service.
