What this is

Finanțe is a personal, non-commercial budgeting application operated by Gabriel Florea for private use by himself and his partner. It is not a public service, not offered for sale, and not available for registration. Access is by invitation only.

What it does

It helps two people plan how to split a monthly income between shared costs, savings goals and personal spending, and shows what was actually spent. Where a user connects a bank account, it reads account and transaction information read-only through Enable Banking, a licensed PSD2 account information service provider.

The application does not hold money, does not move money, and cannot initiate payments. It is a planning tool.

Accounts

Bank connections

Connecting a bank account is optional. It requires the user to authenticate directly with their own bank and to give explicit consent, which expires periodically and can be withdrawn at any time, both in the application and at the bank.
